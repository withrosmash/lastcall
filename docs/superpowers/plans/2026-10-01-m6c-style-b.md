# M6c: Style B in — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The app's avatar becomes Claude Design's Style B (64 × 80). It does everything today's avatar does, then gains the new wardrobe, unlocks and effects.

**Architecture:** Claude Design's `avatar-draw.js` becomes a pure ES module, `js/avatar-art.js`, that draws a look as separate layers. `js/avatar.js` keeps its animation half (ANIM, MOODS, toFrame, createAvatar) and replaces its drawing half. Each frame maps the keyframe state to an art look, takes the layers from a cache, and composes them with the frame's part offsets (root, body, head, hair lag, feet). The unlock table moves to a pure data file.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla ES modules, Canvas 2D, `node --test`, Capacitor 8 Android.

**Spec:** the round 4 brief `design/BRIEF-ROUND-4.md` and the hand-back `design/round4/handback/README.md`, plus the owner's decisions of 2026-10-01: 64 × 80 grid; Crew parked until the domain; the unlock table below, which the owner reviews with this plan.

## Global Constraints

- **House rules:**
  - no facial hair;
  - the avatar is never shown drunk, and never holds alcohol (the drink reaction holds the art's soft-drink `cup`, never an amber glass);
  - the resting smile is two corners, then a line;
  - costumes never hide the face.
- Heavy, tired, half or sleepy eyes never pair with a smile, cat or wide mouth.
- **Unlocks:**
  - nothing unlocks from a drinking badge (french-exit, one-and-done, mixologist, brand-loyal, balanced-books, metronome);
  - glasses are free and sunglasses are earned (First Dare);
  - unlocks are read from earned badges and never stored.
- **Sizes stay as today.** A CSS cell of half today's value, snapped to whole device pixels: `deviceCell = max(1, round(cell × devicePixelRatio))`. Live screen 1.5, start and walkthrough 2, Customise 2, map walker 1.5.
- **Copy:** sentence case, no long dashes, "challenge" never "dare".
- **Tracking code is not touched.**
- New js files go in the sw.js SHELL. Bump CACHE once, to `lastcall-v35`.
- **Crew is out of scope.** The group code stays in `design/round4/handback/` and doesn't ship.

### Task 2: The engine draws Style B

**Files:**
- Modify: `js/avatar.js` (catalogue, `DEFAULT_LOOK`, `normaliseLook`, `build`, `drawStill`, `paintAvatar`, `mini`, `drawMini`, `createAvatar` sizing, `CROP`).
- Test: `tests/avatar.test.mjs` (rewrite the drawing tests, keep the animation ones).

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1 exports.
- Produces (callers keep these names): `normaliseLook(saved) → look v3`, `build(look, frame, only?) → Array(W*H)` of `{c:[r,g,b], z}` or null, `toFrame` (unchanged), `createAvatar({cell, look, onTap, label})` (same methods), `drawStill(canvas, look, {scale, crop, face, only, silhouette, fit})`, `paintAvatar(g, look, x0, y0, s, face) → {w, h}`, `drawMini(canvas, look, {frame, flip, scale})`, `CROP` in 64 × 80 space, and `W`, `H`, `MW`, `MH` re-exported.
- **Look v3:** `{ v: 3, hair, top, glasses, hat, held, costume, shoes, scarf, backpack, effect, colors: { skin, hair, eyes, cheeks, top, bottoms, shoes, glasses, hat } }`. The mode touch stays a draw-time field `touch`, which is passed to the art as `mode`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests**
  - `a v2 look moves to v3 without losing anything`:
    - quiff becomes short, bucket becomes sunhat, and colours are kept;
    - a panda, dino or duck saved in `hat` moves to `costume`, because the new art draws them as costumes;
    - `glasses` and `hat` colours are filled from the art defaults;
    - unknown values fall back to the art defaults.
  - `the default look is Style B's default`.
  - `every keyframe of every animation maps to names the art knows`. For each `__ANIM` frame, `artState(toFrame(st))` has arms in `ARMS`, eyes in `EYES`, mouth in `MOUTHS`, and a prop in `HELD_ITEMS` or null.
  - `never drunk`: no keyframe and no mood base pairs eyes in {half, sleepy, heavy, tired} with a mouth in {smile, cat, wide}, and the drink reaction's prop is `cup`.
  - `layers are cached`: two frames with the same face and arms call the art's `build` once (count through an injected builder).
  - `the frame offsets move whole parts`: `headDY: -1` moves a head pixel up one row and leaves the feet where they were.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run, expect FAIL.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.**
  - `artState(frame)` maps today's vocabulary to the art's:
    - `wave` → `wave1`, `closed` → `shut`, `half` and `sleepy` → `heavy`, `up` → `lookUp`;
    - `determined` → `open` with `raised` brows;
    - a `prop` `['pin', x, y]` becomes the art's `pin` drawn loose, offset by (x, y) scaled by 64/32;
    - `glass` → `cup`.
  - Cache built layers in an LRU of 48, keyed by look plus pose, arms, held and face.
  - Compose with groups:
    - **root:** everything;
    - **body:** body, mode, arms, held, grip and packBack, offset by `bodyDY`;
    - **head:** head, face, glasses, hat and hairFront, offset by `headDX`/`headDY`, with the hair layers trailing by `hairLag`;
    - **feet:** footL/footR offsets, with `legWalk` choosing the art's walk frames.
  - Redraw the extras the art lacks at the new scale, on an `fx` layer: zzz, the music note, the burst, the badge rise and the glass tap.
  - Snap the fractional `cell` to device pixels as in Global Constraints.
  - `paintAvatar` and `drawStill` use the composed grid.
  - The walker uses the art's `mini`.
  - New `CROP`: head [6, 0, 52, 50], body [6, 44, 52, 30], held [30, 40, 32, 32], shoes [10, 66, 44, 14]. Adjust in Task 3 if tiles clip.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run, expect PASS.** Run the whole suite, then time a 60-frame reaction in the browser (median frame under 4 ms on desktop).
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** ("Avatar: Style B in the animation engine").
