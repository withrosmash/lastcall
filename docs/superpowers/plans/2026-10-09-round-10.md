# Round 10: honest routes, cards and the history map. Implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Act on the owner's 2026-10-09 field test: stop calling time indoors "dropped", leave transport out of a walk's distance and pace, add the map behind the recap trace and a period map on History, make the photo card start as the route card, add hide start and end, ask only for "while using the app" location, and cover the status bar.

**Architecture:** The new route maths lives in `js/state.js` as pure functions over `s.trail` and the session's mode parts. Drawing reuses `js/staticmap.js` (Esri tiles drawn to a canvas) for the recap and History. The route card's layout is pulled out into one function, and the photo card uses the same function for its default positions.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla ES modules, Capacitor 8 Android, `node --test`.

**Spec:** the owner's messages of 2026-10-09 (in this session) and docs/ROADMAP.md item P1.

## Global Constraints

- No long dashes in user-facing copy; sentence case; "challenge", never "dare"; not a drinking app.
- Never change native tracking code or the plugin's start options (`distanceFilter: 25`, `requestPermissions: true`).
- Transport cut-off: **20 km/h**, measured over at least **60 s**. Gap: no fix for more than 12 min **and** the next fix more than **250 m** away.
- Walk leaves transport out of distance and pace. Night out, Day out and Festival count every metre. The trace is always drawn in full.
- Route card and recap: the whole line faint, the counted stretches in mint, but only when something was left out. Photo card: the trace as now.
- Hide start and end trims **200 m** from each end. **Off by default.** Cards only; the recap, History and distance keep the full route.
- Bump the service worker `CACHE` (sprell-v43 → sprell-v44) for any change to a shipped file.

## Review Focus

1. A walk with no transport at all must keep exactly today's distance, pace and plain mint line (no faint layer).
2. A night out with a walk part: only transport inside the walk's time span is left out.
3. Hide start and end on a route shorter than 400 m: there is nothing left to draw, so the route is left off rather than drawing a stray dot.
4. History map with the period holding no routes: no map box and no Explore button.
5. Old sessions without `parts` read as Night out, so nothing about them changes.

### Task 1: Honest gaps and the counted distance

**Files:** Modify `js/state.js`, `js/session.js` (gapNote, tiles), `js/history.js`, `js/map.js`, `js/festival.js`, `js/badge-checks.js`, `js/card.js:791`, `js/wardrobe.js:124`. Test: `tests/state.test.mjs`.

**Interfaces (produces):**
- `GAP_AWAY_M = 250`; `trailGaps(s, threshold)` now also requires the haversine between the two fixes to be over `GAP_AWAY_M`.
- `RIDE_KMH = 20`, `RIDE_WINDOW_MS = 60_000`.
- `rideSegments(trail) -> boolean[]`: the entry at index i is for the segment from fix i−1 to fix i (index 0 is always false). A segment is a ride when it is a gap (as above) or when the average speed over the smallest window of fixes around it covering at least 60 s is over 20 km/h. Use the whole trail when the trail is shorter than 60 s.
- `excludedSegments(s, now) -> boolean[]`: `rideSegments` limited to segments whose end time falls inside a walk span (`modeSpans(s, 'walk', now)`).
- `countedDistance(s, now) -> number`: `s.distanceM` minus the metres of the excluded segments, never below 0. Cache it per session and trail length so the live screen doesn't recompute every second.
- `walkPace` leaves out both the excluded segments' metres and their time.
- `summarise(s).distanceM` returns `countedDistance(s)`, and `summarise` also returns `rawDistanceM: s.distanceM`.
- Every reader of `s.distanceM` listed under Files switches to `S.countedDistance(s)` or `summarise`.

- [ ] Tests:
  - `a long stay indoors is not a gap`: 3 h with no fix, next fix 40 m away → `trailGaps` is `[]`.
  - `a jump far away is a gap`: 30 min, 3 km → one gap.
  - `a walk at 5 km/h has no rides`: fixes every 20 s, 28 m apart → all false.
  - `a train at 60 km/h is a ride`: 10 fixes 20 s apart, 333 m apart, inside a walk → true from index 1 to 9.
  - `one GPS jump is not a ride`: a walk at 5 km/h with one fix 80 m off course → no rides (averaged over 60 s).
  - `walk distance leaves the train out`: 1 km walk + 5 km train + 1 km walk, all Walk → `countedDistance` is about 2000 (±5).
  - `a night out counts the train`: the same trail as Night out → `countedDistance === s.distanceM`.
  - `mixed: only the walk span is trimmed`: train during Night out, then a walk part with its own train → only the second train is left out.
  - `walk pace leaves out train time`: pace equals the walking parts' time over their distance.
- [ ] Run `npm test`: the new tests fail.
- [ ] Implement in `js/state.js`, then switch the readers. In the recap's `gapNote`, after the gap line, Walk gets a caption when something was left out: `"{x} km on transport isn't counted in your walk."` (only when x ≥ 0.1).
- [ ] `npm test` all green, then commit "Route: gaps only when you reappear elsewhere; walks leave transport out".
