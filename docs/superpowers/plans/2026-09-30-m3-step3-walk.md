# M3 step 3: Walk — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Walk becomes the third mode, with its own buttons, headline numbers (steps, distance, pace) and six badges.

**Architecture:** Walk is one more entry in `MODES`. The live screen gains a `more` button key and per-mode labels, and the stat tiles follow `MODES[mode].headline`. Walk badges run on `S.sliceTo(s, 'walk')`, like Day out and Night out. The distance of a slice is summed from its trail, because `distanceM` is kept only for the whole adventure.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-30-m2-modes-design.md` (build order step 3).

## Global Constraints

- The spec's Walk row:
  - buttons: Hydrate, Snack, Check in, Challenge, Map, with Drink under More;
  - drinks: `Coffee, Tea, Soft drink, Pint, Low/no`;
  - headline: steps, distance, pace;
  - morning-after: no.
- Walk entry: key `walk`, label `Walk`, Lucide `footprints` icon, picker hint `Parks, paths, the long way round.`, drink hint `Oat latte`.
- Pace is minutes per kilometre over the whole elapsed time, shown as `m:ss /km`. Below 200 m it reads `Not yet`. Never a dash character.
- Walk badges (accents from the round 3 delivery), category `Walk`, no item unlocks:

| slug | name | criteria | accent | rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `early-riser` | Early Riser | Start a walk between 4am and 8am | amber | walk slice `startedAt` hour in [4, 8) |
| `trailblazer` | Trailblazer | 15 km in one walk | forest | walk-slice trail distance ≥ 15 000 m |
| `tea-break` | Tea Break | Coffee or tea on a walk | mint | a walk-slice drink matching coffee or `tea` |
| `head-space` | Head Space | A walk on your own | mint | a walk part with company `solo` |
| `weekly-walker` | Weekly Walker | A walk in 4 weeks running | forest | walk adventures in 4 consecutive Monday-start weeks |
| `out-and-about` | Out and About | 10 walks all-time | forest | ≥ 10 adventures with a walk part |

## Review Focus

1. **Early Riser at 2am.** A walk started at 2am (walking home) doesn't count. It needs [4, 8). (test)
2. **A switched adventure.** Trailblazer only counts kilometres walked during the walk part. (test)
3. **Weeks across a year boundary.** Weekly Walker counts weeks by their Monday date, not by week number, so 28 Dec to 18 Jan works. (test)
4. **Pace on a zero-distance walk,** for example with GPS off, reads `Not yet` and never divides by zero. (test)
5. **The More sheet** on a walk opens the Walk drink list, and the drink logs to the walk part. (browser)
