# Phone checklist (M6 build)

Things only a real phone can show. Tick each one on the APK from CI. If something's off, note the step number.

## First launch
Clear the app's data first (Settings › Apps › Leit › Storage › Clear storage). This also wipes your history, so export it from Settings first.

1. Five steps. The second shows four kinds of adventure. Tapping each one dresses the avatar: camera, backpack straps, wristband and glitter. Night out adds nothing.
2. Location still opens Android's settings. Pick "Allow all the time", then come back.
3. Steps asks for Physical activity. Notifications asks once.
4. Import your history again (Settings › Import history).

## Start and live screen
5. The wordmark on the start screen has clear space above and below.
6. The gear and badges icons in the top corner open Settings and Badges, before and during an adventure.
7. Start a walk. Pace says "Not yet" for the first two minutes, then a sensible number that keeps updating without tapping anything.
8. Log a snack on the walk. The toast says "Snack logged".
9. Switch from with friends to on my own without changing mode. The toast says "Now on your own."
10. Switch to Festival. The map button shows Sets, and there's no Drop pin, only "Back to the adventure".

## Location off
11. Turn location off and check in. You can still type a name, and the stop counts. The map later doesn't show it in the wrong place.
12. No zero tiles. Distance and pace are hidden, not shown as 0.

## After
13. A short finished walk shows "Too short" for pace, not "Not yet".
14. Make a festival from two days tapped in reverse order. The days list in date order, and the name uses the first day.
15. Leave a festival review with one day. It says "1 day".
16. Export a festival day's route as GPX. The file has the sets as waypoints.
17. The route card's date line has a gap above the wordmark.

## Settings
18. Bigger text makes everything larger, on top of the phone's own font size, and nothing gets cut off.
19. Calmer avatar stops the idle moves. Reactions still play.
20. Turn the phone's font size to the largest. Your numbers still fits above the navigation bar.

## TalkBack (optional)
21. With TalkBack on, the live timer reads its time, sheets read their title first, and closing a sheet goes back to the button that opened it.
