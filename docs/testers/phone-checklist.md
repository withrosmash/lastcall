# Phone checklist (build 988e9c3)

Things only a real phone can show. The tickable version is the Leit phone checklist artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/WmDr4Z74iExSoxxss31sLG); this file mirrors it.

## First launch
Step 1 wipes the app. Export your history first: Settings › Export history.

1. Clear the app’s data: phone Settings › Apps › Leit › Storage › Clear storage. Then open Leit.
2. The walkthrough has five steps. The second shows four kinds of adventure, and tapping each dresses the avatar: a camera, backpack straps, a wristband with glitter. Night out adds nothing.
3. The location step opens Android’s settings. Pick “Allow all the time”, then come back.
4. Steps asks for Physical activity. Notifications asks once.
5. Import your history again: Settings › Import history.

## Look

6. The home screen icon is the white LEIT wordmark on forest green, crisp, and inside a circle if your launcher uses round icons.
7. Your avatar has the same colours and glasses in its new style. A Quiff is now Short, and a bucket hat is now the sun hat.
8. On the start screen, the wordmark has clear space above and below.
9. The gear and badges icons in the top corner open Settings and Badges, before and during an adventure.

## On an adventure

10. Start a walk. Pace says “Not yet” for the first two minutes, then a sensible number with “/km” that keeps updating on its own.
11. Log a snack on the walk. The message says “Snack logged”.
12. Switch from with friends to on my own, keeping the same mode. The message says “Now on your own.”
13. Switch to Festival. The map shows Sets and has no Drop pin, only “Back to the adventure”.
14. Log a drink, a water, food and a check in. Each reaction plays smoothly, including the first. The drink is a soft drink with a straw, and water is a glass of water.

## Location off

15. With location off, check in. You can still type a name, the stop counts, and it never appears on the map in the wrong place.
16. No tiles show zero for things the phone couldn’t measure. Distance and pace are hidden.

## After an adventure

17. A short finished walk shows “Too short” for pace.
18. Make a festival from two days, tapping the later day first. The days list in date order, and the name uses the first day.
19. A festival review with one day left says “1 day”.
20. Export a festival day’s route as GPX. The file has the sets as waypoints.
21. On the route card, the date line has a gap above the wordmark.
22. End an adventure that earns a badge with an item. The unlock sheet shows the item, and Wear it puts it on.
23. On a history map, the walker matches your look and walks the route.

## Customise

24. There are tabs for Hats, Costumes, Hold, Extras and Effects. Locked items show as grey shapes with the badge that unlocks them.
25. Wear a costume and an effect. Both show on the live screen and on a share card.
26. Put on a costume while wearing a cap. The costume’s own hat replaces the cap.
27. Wearing sunglasses, earn a badge or finish a challenge. The glasses go up on the head so the star or heart eyes show.

## Settings

28. Bigger text makes everything larger, on top of the phone’s own font size, and nothing gets cut off.
29. Calmer avatar stops the idle moves and holds effects still. Reactions still play.
30. With the phone’s font size at its largest, Your numbers still fits above the navigation bar.

## TalkBack (optional)

31. With TalkBack on, the live timer reads its time, sheets read their title first, and closing a sheet goes back to the button that opened it.
