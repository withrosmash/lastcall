# Questions for testers

A message ready to paste into the group chat. It went through a Humanizer pass. Send it after testers have had the build for a week or two.

Hey, thanks for testing Leit. A few quick questions, and short answers are fine.

First, could you open Leit, go to History, then Settings, then Your numbers, take a screenshot and send it to me? It only shows numbers and dates, and they stay on your phone until you send them. If you haven't finished an adventure yet, just say so.

1. What did you use it for? A night out, a day out, a walk, a festival, something else?
2. Did you use it more than once? If you stopped, what stopped you?
3. Did you share a card anywhere? If not, why not?
4. What was the best bit: the avatar, a challenge, a badge, the recap, the map?
5. What annoyed you or didn't work?
6. Was anything confusing, like starting, switching mode or finding your history?
7. How was your battery by the end?
8. Is there an outing you'd use it for that it doesn't handle well yet?
9. Would you tell a friend about it? What would you say?

Thank you. Every answer helps.
