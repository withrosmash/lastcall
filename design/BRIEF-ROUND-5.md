# Sprell: Claude Design brief, round 5

Leit is being renamed **Sprell**. It's a Norwegian word for lively antics or a caper ("sprell levende" means alive and kicking), and it's said "sprel", to rhyme with "spell". The app is the same: adventures with your crew or on your own, mainly nights out, also days out, walks and festivals. Your round 2 tokens, round 3 badges and round 4 avatar all stay as they are.

The owner found the round 3 "Norr" wordmark too harsh: the square corners, hard stroke ends and very wide spacing. They chose a rounded, friendly, lower-case direction. Bold, bubbly lower case (like Fredoka) came a close second.

Please keep sentence-case labels, and use no long dashes in any copy.

## 1. The Sprell wordmark

- **The word:** "sprell", in lower case.
- **The direction:** the owner picked a mock-up set in Comfortaa (Google Fonts, weight 600, letter-spacing about 0.06em). Please draw a custom wordmark in that spirit rather than setting the font:
  - round, geometric, friendly, a little retro;
  - clearly "playful night-out cool", never childish;
  - must sit happily beside the 64 by 80 pixel avatar and the round 2 system type (system-ui).
- **Watch for:** the double "ll" should read as two letters at small sizes (the home screen icon is about 48px); "sp" at the start shouldn't clump.
- **Not a drinking app:** nothing in the mark should suggest drinks, glasses, bubbles from a drink or anything boozy.
- **Where it goes:**
  - the start screen, above "Track the adventure. Piece it together later.";
  - the share cards (route and photo, dark and light text themes);
  - the walkthrough's first step;
  - the app icon (below) and the Play Store feature graphic (1024 by 500).
- **Please send:** 2 or 3 versions to choose from, then the chosen one as SVG in white, dark and mono, with the clear space and a minimum size.

## 2. The app icon

- Today's icon is the white wordmark on forest green (#114530). The owner liked that green (the darkest of three options).
- Please send the icon with the new wordmark as an Android adaptive icon (foreground and background layers, 108dp with the 72dp safe zone), a round and a square preview, the monochrome layer for themed icons, and a 1024px master for the stores.
- If the full word gets too small at icon size, offer a version with just a mark (an "s" or a shape from the wordmark) as an alternative, so the owner can compare.

## 3. Store graphics

- The Play Store feature graphic (1024 by 500, 24-bit PNG, no transparency) and a store lockup of icon plus wordmark.
