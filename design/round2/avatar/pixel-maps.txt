LAST CALL AVATAR: PIXEL MAPS (round 2)

Grid: 32 x 43, head centre (16,17), eyes at (12,19) and (20,19). Map sprite: 12 x 16.
One character per pixel. '.' is empty.
Slot letters: S skin, H hair and brows, E eyes, C cheeks, T top, B bottoms, F shoes.
Lowercase is a darker tone of that slot (outline or shade). + * ~ ^ = - _ are lighter tones (highlights) of skin, hair, eyes, cheeks, top, bottoms and shoes.
The engine derives both from the slot colour: outline x0.58, shade x0.86, highlight mixed 32% toward white.
Outlines on near-black slot colours lighten instead (mixed 32% toward #969696).
Digits and other characters are fixed colours, listed under each map.
Layers drawn alone have outlines on every open edge. When worn, the engine only outlines edges against empty space or lower layers.
The drawing code for every layer is in avatar-draw.js, which is the source of truth.
