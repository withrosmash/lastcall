# Leit: Claude Design brief, round 3

Last Call is now called **Leit** (pronounced like "late"). It's for any adventure, not only nights out: when you start, you pick Night out, Day out, Walk or Festival. Round 2 (your tokens, avatar, badges and screens) is built and in testers' hands. This round is small, just two pieces, because the app screens for the new modes are being built directly in the app on your round 2 system.

Please keep everything from round 2: the tokens, the dark and light grounds, the badge template, and sentence-case labels. No long dashes in any copy.

## 1. The Leit wordmark

- **The word:** "Leit". Icelandic for "search" or "quest". It's said like "late", so the lettering shouldn't make people read it as "light" or "leet".
- **The feel:** keep the Scandinavian, mythic feel the name was chosen for, and still sit happily beside the pixel avatar and the round 2 type. Friendly, not corporate.
- **Where it goes:**
  - the start screen, above "Track the adventure. Piece it together later.";
  - the foot of the share card (route and photo cards, dark and light);
  - the splash hand-off frame, under a 160px avatar disc;
  - a store lockup with the app icon (the icon itself stays direction A from round 2).
- **Please send:** 2 or 3 directions to choose from, then the chosen one as SVG in dark and light, with a mono version.

## 2. Eighteen new badges

Same template as the 38 you drew: disc, ring, glyph, glow and accent. Please send each one in dark and light. There are no locked versions; the app greys locked badges itself. Please strip editor metadata from the SVGs.

**Day out**

| Name | Earned by |
|---|---|
| Day Into Night | switching an adventure from Day out to Night out |
| Tourist | 6+ stops in one day out |
| Explorer | 3 stops you've never been to before |
| Brunch Club | food before 11am |
| Caffeine Trail | 3 coffees in one day out |
| Sunday Best | a day out on a Sunday |

**Walk**

| Name | Earned by |
|---|---|
| Early Riser | a walk started before 8am |
| Trailblazer | 15 km in one walk |
| Tea Break | coffee or tea on a walk |
| Head Space | a walk on your own |
| Weekly Walker | a walk in 4 weeks running |
| Out and About | 10 walks all-time |

**Festival**

| Name | Earned by |
|---|---|
| Front Row | 5 sets in one day |
| Headliner | a set after 10pm |
| Stage Hopper | sets from 3 spots at least 200m apart |
| Hydration Station | 5 waters in a festival day |

| Full Weekend | a festival of 3+ days |

**Accents:** Day out, Walk and Festival each get their own glow colour in the app, and those colours are being picked now. So please use the existing accent set (mint, pink, amber, forest) for the badges. We'll send the three glow colours as soon as they're chosen, in case you'd like to use them instead.

**Also:** the badge "First Night" is renamed **First Adventure**. It's a name change only; the art stays.

## Not in this round
