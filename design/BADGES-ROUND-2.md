# Round 2 badges: addendum for Claude Design

Six new badges, same spec as `BADGES.md` and the challenge badges before them:
SVG, 512 canvas, 88% safe zone, 1.5-unit stroke on a 24 grid, earned and locked
states, and one accent from mint `#7EE0C0`, pink `#F06C9B`, amber `#EF9F27` or
forest `#35A26F`. Each one unlocks an avatar item from the round 2 handoff.

Rule, unchanged: nothing unlocks from a drinking badge, and none of these
should read as boozy.

| Slug | Name | Criteria | Unlocks | Accent | Category |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `long-haul` | Long Haul | Walk 50 km, all-time | Cowboy hat | forest | Aspirational |
| `just-add-water` | Just Add Water | Log 50 waters, all-time | Duck hood | mint | Health |
| `snack-break` | Snack Break | Log food three times in one night | Panda hood | amber | Funny |
| `big-stomp` | Big Stomp | 20,000 steps in one night | Dinosaur hood | forest | Health |
| `late-bite` | Late Bite | Log food after midnight | Pizza slice | amber | Funny |
| `anniversary` | Anniversary | One year since your first night | Balloon | mint | Streaks |

Symbol ideas, only as starting points: a signpost or long winding path (Long
Haul), a glass with a wave in it (Just Add Water), a sandwich (Snack Break), a
big footprint (Big Stomp), a crescent moon with a slice (Late Bite), a candle or
a "1" (Anniversary).

## Changes to the round 2 proposal

- **"Left the Building" is not needed.** Five stops in one night is already the
  existing **Pin Cushion** badge, so Pin Cushion unlocks the Elvis costume.
- **"Nine Lives" is dropped.** It unlocked the cat ears, which are now free
  from install, along with the mug.

## Light versions: not needed

The app makes the light-mode badges itself by swapping colours. Every existing
badge uses the same template, so the swap is exact:

| Part | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| Disc | `#141414` | `#FFFFFF` with a `#D5DDE9` hairline |
| Mint | `#7EE0C0` | `#0B6E55` |
| Pink | `#F06C9B` | `#C92F68` |
| Amber | `#EF9F27` | `#A35F00` |
| Forest | `#35A26F` | `#21764F` |
| Highlight arc | white at 6% | navy `#0B1526` at 6% |

Glows and rings keep their opacity and take the new accent. **Please build the
six new badges on the same template** (one accent, the same disc, ring, glow
and highlight structure) so the swap works for them too. If you'd like the light
treatment changed, send one example per accent and the rule gets updated.
